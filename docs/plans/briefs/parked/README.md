name. Not shipped by anything; `decoration_paths` does not look here.

  temple, guard ferrule, black static). Replaced on 2026-09-14 by the Ender Dragon's scale-horns;
  the owner called this one "a good piece, not here". Candidate for a beast or a culture pack.
