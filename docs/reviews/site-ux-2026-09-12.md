# armorpieces.com: authoring and sharing, a UX review

**Date:** 2026-09-12, two passes the same day. **Reviewed:** ArmorPiecesSite `3662d20` (working tree
clean), plugin `tools/blockbench_plugin/armorpieces.js` in both the working copy and the published
clone, ArmorPiecesBlockbench `web/`, and for the second pass the mod working copy (0.3.0 released,
0.4.0 unreleased) and ArmorPiecesDiscord. **Method:** first pass, four parallel reviews (the live

Every "broken" claim in both
passes was then re-verified in the source by hand. **Nothing was changed.** The screenshots and the

## Verdict

Browsing and downloading the mod's own pack works well. The authoring path has four flows that are
genuinely broken and a long tail of friction: a new author today cannot get from "I made a piece"
to "it is in the gallery" without hitting a dead end, and a player cannot tell which mod version a
download needs.

The second pass found that the first told about half the story. The other half is worse, because
it is the half nobody has walked yet: **an outfit can never be published** (no page sends the
request), **a maintainer reviews a submission without its bytes or a picture** (the queue's own
Download strips the pieces under review), **the wiki's search is dead on the live site** (the CSP
refuses Pagefind's WebAssembly), **the wiki documents an unreleased mod** (synced from `main`, it
teaches 0.4.0 commands to 0.3.0 players and disagrees with the gallery by five skins), and
**nothing in the mod, the README header or the mod manifest points at the site at all**. Around
those: takedowns of packs and outfits that the owner silently undoes, an account deletion that
does not delete the account, a linked repository whose pieces become yours, a privacy page that
understates what is stored, and every error page a line of bare monospace text.

## Is this the whole story?

The lifecycle from finding the mod to leaving the site, and where each stage is reviewed.

| Being reviewed; being told the outcome | author | The maintainer | no notification of anything |

**Not reviewed, and why.** The live OAuth sign-ins (they cannot be exercised offline; the test
route stands in for them). The Modrinth and CurseForge project pages as configured on the stores

Blockbench plugin's modelling and painting surface itself (it has its own bridge and gate work).
The mod's in-game screens (the site is the subject). The Discord bot (no server exists yet).

## Cross-cutting: previews, keyboard, weight

**Social previews and discovery [src].** `Base.astro:27-35` emits title, description,
color-scheme and icon, nothing else: no Open Graph, no Twitter card, no canonical. A piece link
pasted into Discord or Reddit shows no picture although every object has a 256 px thumbnail and
every outfit a render. No `robots.txt`, no sitemap (the comment at `astro.config.mjs:14` names one;
none is built), no feed of releases or packs. `astro.config.mjs:15` defaults `site` to
`https://armorpieces.example`; whether the deploy sets `SITE_URL` is not in the repo [inf].

**Keyboard and screen readers [src][walk2].** Skip link present and first in the tab order; every
stop has a focus ring. The sheet is `role=dialog aria-modal`, takes focus, closes on Escape,
returns focus to the opener and honours reduced motion (verified in the walk on both widths). The
Packs / Pieces toggle is anchors with `role="tab"` inside `role="tablist"` (`gallery/index.astro:78-80`):
the role promises arrow keys it does not have. The hero slider auto-advances every 6 s, stops on
hover, focus, touch and hidden tab, skips itself under reduced motion, but has no visible pause
control. Alt text is right throughout. Contrast passes everywhere, computed from `global.css:5-32`
(accent on background 8.25:1, muted text 6.57:1, dark theme 7.18:1 and 7.02:1).

**Weight [src].** `/`: five hero PNGs at 1920×1080, 1.75 MB together, the first eager and the rest
lazy, but the autoplay brings each in within 6 s; then three outfit cards each stacking up to twelve
256 px thumbs under the render. `/gallery/?view=pieces`: 107 PNG thumbs, ~25 KB each, 2.7 MB in
all, lazy, no WebP, no `srcset`. Every wiki page loads `pagefind-ui.js` 120 KB + CSS 14 KB before
anyone searches. The editor does not preload Pyodide (`armorpieces.js:432-433` imports it on the
first piece); the 8.3 MB Blockbench bundle is the price of the page.
