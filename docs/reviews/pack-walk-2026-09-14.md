**Verdict on the walked eight: verified.** Every edited piece was saved through the plugin,
`check_authoring.py` is clean on each pack, `check_additive.py` finds no id twice, and
`check_part.py` on the pack's own sheets is clean except where the owner cut faces on purpose
(dragon_wings: four `down` faces and the static/inlay texels under them - a `!` the bridge would
refuse to save past, left as designed).

**What the walk taught the tools** (LESSONS #29, `coplanar-check.md`): a fan of plates rotated
about one axis must step along that axis or every plate shares two planes; the check is blind to
it (no planes for rotated cubes, no self-comparison). A person sees it in the viewport in
seconds - which is the argument for this kind of walk.
